A powerful Quality of Life (QoL) mod that introduces real-time inventory searching, dynamic smart categories, and a custom filter creator with color highlights to drastically improve your inventory management experience.

[h2]Features & How It Works[/h2]

The vanilla Encumbrance icon now has a brand new function: [b]Filtering![/b]
[img]https://i.imgur.com/JRj78qD.png[/img]

Clicking that icon reveals a new [b]Filter[/b] option.
[img]https://i.imgur.com/d0jV4XP.png[/img]

From here, you get access to three main menus: 
- [b]Zomboid:[/b] Automatically generates categories based ONLY on the items currently inside the container (fully compatible with other mods!).
- [b]Player:[/b] Allows you to create, edit, and apply your own custom filters.
- [b]Clear Filters:[/b] Instantly removes any active filter.
[img]https://i.imgur.com/tDxLkUN.png[/img]

[b]The Zomboid tab dynamically organizing a messy container with various items:[/b]
[img]https://i.imgur.com/fCHqZP4.png[/img]

[h3]Custom Player Filters[/h3]
In the Player Tab, you have options to [b]Create a New Filter[/b], [b]Edit a Filter[/b], toggle [b]Highlights[/b], and quickly apply any of your saved filters.
[img]https://i.imgur.com/sc0fZV0.png[/img]

[b]Creating a New Filter:[/b]

In this menu, you can build your custom filter by adding specific categories or exact items. 
[i]Tip: You can drag and drop items straight from your inventory into the search bar to add them instantly![/i]
Finally, pick a custom [b]Highlight Color[/b] and choose whether the filter should apply to categories, specific items, or both.
[img]https://i.imgur.com/0hVcYSE.png[/img]

[b]Managing Your Filters:[/b]

Easily edit or delete your existing filters.
